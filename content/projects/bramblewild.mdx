# Project overview

I develop The Vanishing of Bramblewild using Unity and Dreamscape during my IT internship at TUHSD. The project began with a six-scene lesson plan and story about collecting strong and weak evidence and using evidence to support arguments.

## My contributions

- **Requirements and planning:** I translated the lesson plan into user stories, functional and nonfunctional requirements, and incremental milestones and implementation slices.
- **Experience development:** I developed the experience and assets with AI assistance.
- **Desktop stakeholder demo:** In my first month, I showed stakeholders a desktop demo of Scenes 0–1, with narration, UI interactions, and student-choice gates.
- **Dreamscape integration:** I coordinated DDK setup with the Dreamscape team and migrated the prior project into the current Dreamscape environment.
- **Automated tests:** I wrote edit-mode and play-mode tests with the Unity Test Framework.

## Current stage

My internship began in May 2026 and is ongoing. The desktop demo has been shown to stakeholders. The first in-lab demo has also been presented to stakeholders, and their feedback was positive. Release is planned for January 2027.
