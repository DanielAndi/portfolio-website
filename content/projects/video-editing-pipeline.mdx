# Project overview

CreatorOS is an internal Bavolar Studios tool that I actively use for my YouTube channel's gameplay-video workflow. Its current implementation is a configurable video-processing pipeline, with ongoing development guided by that use.

Bavolar Studios brings together my YouTube channels, freelance development, and work on games, apps, and services.

## Engineering approach

- **Python CLI and YAML profiles** configure processing stages for different editing workflows.
- **Staged processing** separates ingest, audio cleanup, transcription, clip selection, timeline assembly, and rendering.
- **FFmpeg** handles media processing; optional Whisper integrations support transcription.
- **Per-job workspaces** organize intermediate artifacts and logs by stage, making individual runs easier to inspect.
- **Optional narrative assistance** and review workflows keep creator decisions part of the editing process.

## Current use

The tool is in active internal use for my channel while development continues. That workflow helps guide improvements to the editing process.

## Future direction

I want to develop CreatorOS into a monetized product. The longer-term goal is to help creators turn gameplay or livestream recordings into videos with the quality and publishing frequency associated with a production team. That is a product goal, not a demonstrated outcome of the current pipeline.
